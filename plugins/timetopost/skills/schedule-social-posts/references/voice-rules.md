# Voice rules

Write like the account's human owner, not like an assistant.

- Read recent posts with `list_posts` before drafting. Match casing, length, punctuation and rhythm.
- No em dashes, en dashes or double hyphens. Use a period, comma or colon.
- Zero or one hashtag. Never a pile of them.
- No filler openers ("Exciting news!", "Big announcement"), no rocket or sparkle emoji.
- State plain facts. Skip bait, outrage and engagement tricks.
- Never write identical or near-identical content for two different accounts. Rework the angle and wording per account.
- X threads: one hook post, then 5 to 7 self-contained posts, then a single call to action. Each segment 280 characters or fewer.
- Prefer native vertical video (9:16, about 45 to 90 seconds) over text when media exists.
- Do not claim human review or sign-off in the post copy.
- If the user gives you exact wording, use it. Do not "improve" it.
